Mornfolk · Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026. Draft for App Store review.

Mornfolk ("we") is a morning kindness app. We collect as little as possible.

What we store to deliver a morning

To match mornings in a time zone, the server keeps a device key created on your phone. It is not your name, email, phone number, Apple ID, or Google account.

We store the morning you leave (words from our closed list, or a gesture we wrote), an optional city you choose yourself, and reactions you send. Mornings disappear when that morning ends.

We do not store whether someone opened a shared postcard link. That would be a read receipt. Names and marks you give to people you know stay only on your device.

Analytics

We use AppMetrica (Yandex) to understand whether people return and which screens they open.

  • Signals use an anonymous device profile for guests.
  • We never send names, free text you typed, emails, phone numbers, or advertising identifiers that we invent ourselves.
  • Analytics are not used to sell ads or track you across other apps.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple or Google. We learn only whether a purchase is active, never your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, a push token can be stored so we can tell you a morning arrived. You can turn notifications off in system settings.

Children

Mornfolk is for ages 13+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Write to us from the app settings if you want a morning removed.
  • Delete the app to remove local names, marks, and the diary on that phone.
