See the morning

01 Ritual

Three quiet steps.

  • Choose how your morning feels. Then build a short phrase from our words, or pass on warm hands.
  • Leave yours. Receive theirs. Your morning goes to someone in your time zone. Theirs opens for you.
  • Answer with a reaction — or close the day. When morning ends, the exchange ends with it.

02 Promise

What we will not do.

  • No read receipts for people you know. Opening a shared morning never tells the sender you looked.
  • Receiving stays free. You never pay to open warmth. Optional purchases are for gifts and keepsakes.
  • No names, no chase. The app is not a messenger and not a dating feed.

03 Trust

Little data. Clear doors.

We keep a device key so a morning can find you. Analytics are anonymous. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone inside the ritual.
